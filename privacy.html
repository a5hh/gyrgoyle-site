Gyrgoyle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Gyrgoyle does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The game does not use advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services, and it makes no network requests.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Saved games and settings are kept only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Motion sensors. The game reads your phone's motion sensors to control the character. This data is used only while you play and never leaves your device.
  • Purchases. Any purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Children. The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
