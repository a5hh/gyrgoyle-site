Gyrgoyle Support

Need a hand? Email ash.apps.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Playing lying down or on the move

Open Settings › Orientation and choose Locked. However you hold the phone becomes "upright". Use Recenter (also in the pause menu) to reset it.

No sound?

Gyrgoyle respects your phone's silent switch. Check it, then check Settings › Audio in the game.

The controls feel too strong or too weak

Settings › Tilt Sensitivity has Low, Medium and High.

Motion sickness or too much movement

Settings › Comfort lets you turn off screen shake, slow motion and haptics.

Bought Wake the Whole City on another device?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
